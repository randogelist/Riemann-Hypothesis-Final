Yes. The current repository is a partial consolidation of a substantially larger research collection. WHAT WE HAD BEYOND THE MAIN REPOSITORY NARRATIVE

1. Actual SOLOBOLO induction and local-pole analysis.

Already retained in the full build: the actual direct Widder formula equals the recursive tower on positive regular anchors, with an unconditional all-order equality at anchor 1/4. The actual Mersenne trace endpoint is also retained. It would be wrong to describe this part as only a manufactured recurrence.

Missing source includes the actual local-pole construction: a literal off-line zeta zero produces an interior simple Cayley pole. This substantially sharpens the forward route. The remaining implication from that pole to eventual negative actual-Widder coefficients is still explicit. Additional missing branches contain real-zeta sign reductions, downward positivity assembly, weak-energy-to-mode convergence, interior mode-root geometry, Wall/Jacobi square decompositions and formal Stieltjes stripping.

2. Actual native Mellin and coefficient-coordinate bridges.

D152 derives the density Mellin identity M(s)=Gamma(s)(s-1)zeta(s) for Re(s)>1 and the actual completed-xi anchor germ. D154 identifies the actual native coefficients with the complex Rodrigues expression at every order. Their source bodies are missing from tracked files. Historical focused native logs support these particular results.

D153's actual energy/tower identification is already archived. Its endpoint still requires both a growth estimate and the actual product-log coefficient identity. The latter has a written symmetric-power/Cauchy derivation, but the inspected Lean sources do not supply the corresponding all-order theorem.

D152 uses a different Mellin transform from the two-variable literal GP measure in the current repository. Its theorem is useful input for that measure, but is not automatically the current Literal.MellinEvaluation theorem.

3. RI01 local analytic convergence and real/complex calibration.

The recovered sources derive actual theta Taylor convergence, uniform convergence of finite determinants on a small disk, and a holomorphic determinant germ. They also identify the real SOLOBOLO logarithmic derivative with the complex theta logarithmic derivative at regular positive anchors. These are substantial missing actual-data results.

They do not construct the finite positive contraction spectra required by SOLOBOLO. Local determinant convergence and convergence of nonnegative trace powers are different statements. A small holomorphic germ also does not give the required global slit-plane or unit-disk continuation.

4. Actual RR binding, rather than only a generic defect model.

D156 already constructs the actual zero-occurrence data, proves summability and nonnegativity of its defect C, and proves C=0 iff native RH. It retains multiplicity through repeated zero occurrences; no simplicity assumption is introduced. This is already preserved in catalogue E23/E24 and an archival source body, with historical native audit evidence.

The binding itself should not remain on a generic “missing” list. What remains is C<=0, or an equivalent all-height bound on the full defect. The available bound controls C-prefix(T); it cannot discard the nonnegative prefix. An RR Hilbert norm representation also does not identify that space with the independently defined GP/H_a space.

5. Constructive GP/Hilbert models and exact payment algebra.

The GNS branch constructs a binary Hilbert-space isometry and positive complementary operators from an all-depth positive-semidefinite kernel lift. A finite atomic branch derives positivity directly from finite features. The exact-ledger branch derives finite telescoping, Gram absorption and reserve-floor estimates. These are reusable constructions missing from the tracked repository.

Their actual inputs remain visible: kernel positivity and anchor calibration in the GNS branch; the analytic debit law, actual readout, reserve-loss payment and strict initial margin in the ledger branch. Neither construction supplies the actual xi calibration merely by being named GP or BKQR.

6. AWPI source chains and later written reductions.

A coherent 23-module direct paired-Phi branch derives native zeta binding on the shifted half-plane, nonvanishing, exact finite residuals and tail decay. Twenty of its module hashes are missing from tracked files even though its terminal criterion is archived.

The recovered Round 5–7 and candidate notes contain additional paper-level mathematics: finite rational-anchor grids at each depth, a normalized inverse-smoothing/logistic-kernel tower, signed polynomial approximants, negative-mass reductions and explicit mode-energy decompositions. The checked finite-grid criterion still has the form “for every K, some later stage k passes all its finite anchor tests.” It is not one finite certificate, and the actual analytic signs are not automatically decidable rational arithmetic.

The direction of positivity matters: the positive convolution kernels propagate positivity downward in depth. The upward differential successor does not preserve positivity merely because the base is positive.

## Independent AWPI estimate checks

Write

`Phi_N(u,s)=4 sum_{m<N} (-1)^m (2m+u+1)^(-s)`,

`T_N(s)=partial_u Phi_N(1,s)`, `T(s)=-s Phi(1,s+1)`, and `sigma=Re(s)>0`.

The Round5 paired-integral argument is valid on compact subsets of `u>-1`, `Re(s)>0`: paired cells are bounded by a summable `O(k^(-1-delta))`, while the u-derivative cells are absolutely locally uniformly summable. It therefore realizes the real-u derivative, and its local complex-u extension, rather than merely declaring the desired derivative formula. The source has not formalized this two-variable family.

The normalization is essential: P23 uses `J=|T|^2` and exact energy `E=J^2=|T|^4`. It does not use `E=|T|^2`.

The omitted derivative terms pair over the disjoint intervals `[X_N+4k,X_N+4k+2]`. Direct integration gives

`|T-T_N| <= 4 |s| |s+1|/(sigma+1) * X_N^(-sigma-1)`.

The index `X_N=2(N+1)` and factor 4 are correct, including odd N. The finite shifted residual is not this derivative-truncation error.

The Round6 quartic bound `||z|^4-|w|^4|<=4B^3|z-w|`, for both norms at most B, gives the stated defect error `O_s(X_N^(-sigma-1))`. Dividing by the **joint** tail `16 X_N^(-2sigma)` leaves `O_s(X_N^(sigma-1))`, which tends to zero at each native zero because the source proves `0<sigma<1`. This is eventually valid at a point-dependent cutoff, not an all-index prescribed-coordinate bound. The height-independent joint tail bound `8/(N+1)` on `sigma>=1/2` does not make the derivative-error cutoff uniform in unbounded height. The paper's plus-channel cofinal construction is valid: choose indices with `tau log(4(N+1))` approaching an integer multiple of pi so that the plus square captures a fixed fraction of the joint tail.

The Round7 Euler-product bounds also check. For `a>1`, factorwise triangle inequalities give

`zeta(2a)/zeta(a) <= |zeta(a+it)| <= zeta(a)`.

With `T(s)=-2s*2^(-s)*(1-2^(-s))*zeta(s+1)`, valid analytically on `sigma>0`, the constants

`L_*=(1-2^(-1/2))/zeta(3/2)` and `U_*=(sqrt(2)+1)*zeta(3/2)`

indeed satisfy `L_*|s|<=|T(s)|<=U_*|s|` for `1/2<=sigma<1`. The sufficient cutoff

`X_N^(3/2) >= 16 |s+1|/(3 L_*)`

therefore gives `|T_N|>=L_*|s|/2` and `E_N>=L_*^4|s|^4/16>0`. This is a useful finite **activity** bound, not finite passivity.

Already `T_1=-2s*2^(-s)` is nonzero at every native zero, so `D_1=(2^(2sigma-1)-1)|T_1|^4<=0` is precisely the one-sided zero-location assertion. This first-prefix observation is not a finite test over a known finite zero set. Likewise, a prescribed negative boundary square tends to zero, whereas the natural derivative defect tends to the actual defect; they cannot be identified just because both have finite definitions.

The reflection identities in Round7 are correct. The normalized sum is `g+1/g-2>=0`, supplying the opposite inequality to the desired upper sign. The unweighted sum retains a shifted-zeta ratio; the zeta functional equation reflects `s+1` to `-s`, not to `2-s`. The paper's both-sign argument concerns ordinary strip points and does not construct off-line zeta zeros. Its recurrence-time argument should explicitly choose positive integer times bounded below by 1; simultaneous pigeonhole approximation then yields an unbounded approximating sequence, since a bounded subsequence would force a common nonzero period for log 2 and log 3. With that detail the analytic argument is sound.

The local implicit-function statement is also consistent: the nonzero u derivative gives a local complex-u graph near each native zero, and the leading coefficient in Round7 equation (16) follows from `u(s)-1=-Phi(1,s)/T(rho)+higher order`. ## Inverse smoothing and logistic story: independent check

The Round6 Green proof is coherent assuming its explicit actual divisor expansion and normal convergence. `v_n=K_(n+1)*v_(n+1)`, `K_m(y)=(m/2)exp(-m|y|)`,

`v_(n+1)=(1-partial_y^2/(n+1)^2)v_n`.

The positive operator runs downward. Positivity of v_0 does not propagate upward through the differential operators.
