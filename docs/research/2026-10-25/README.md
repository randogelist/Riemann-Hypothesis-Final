These are dated source assessments and mathematical proof plans. They are not
native Lean receipts. The authoritative current endpoint is in
[ACTUAL_CONDITION](../../ACTUAL_CONDITION.md); the implementation/build status
is in [RECOVERED_EQUIVALENCES](../../RECOVERED_EQUIVALENCES.md).

- [Three-pass reinference](REINFERENCE_REPORT.txt) records the broader source
  inventory and dependencies, including branches absent from the active build.
- [Positive-product assessment](rh-positive-product-assessment.md) identifies
  existing actual factorization, multiplicity, origin normalization and
  summability results, plus the remaining adapters.
- [Enumeration evidence](product-enumeration-findings.md) details countable
  zero-padding without losing multiplicity.
- [Coefficient bridge](coefficient-bridge-strategy.md) gives the local Taylor
  route from the actual symmetric product to the central Rodrigues formula.

  central Widder coefficients, slit ODE and reciprocal routes.

The earlier endpoint assessment proposes both coefficient-limit and residual
bounds as sufficient input. The new source theorem removes the independent
coefficient-limit hypothesis: locally uniform decay of the actual residual,
with structurally derived holomorphy of every coefficient, suffices. Historical
phrases such as "not pushed" describe the assessment at the time it was written.
They are not the publication status of this Git revision.

The 76-module positive-product support manifest is a research dependency map,
not an active or natively verified 76-module import graph. Source locations in
historical reports identify the inspected archives; they do not imply all those
archives or the supplied manuscript PDF are distributed in this repository.
