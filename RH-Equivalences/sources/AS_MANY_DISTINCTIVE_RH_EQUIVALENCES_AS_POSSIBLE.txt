NORMALIZATION AND TARGET RULES
Native RH means Mathlib _root_.RiemannHypothesis, with trivial zeros and s=1
handled by its actual zero predicate. A project-local Prop named RiemannHypothesis
is classified separately unless an actual native identification is supplied.
The standard entire completion is ξ(s)=(1+s(s−1)completedRiemannZeta₀(s))/2.
Ξ(z)=ξ(1/2+i z); Θ(z)=ξ(1/2+sqrt(z)), with evenness providing the descent.
Raw s(s−1)Λ(s), entire 2ξ(s), and ξ(s) must not be silently interchanged at s=0,1.
Definitions from the cited snapshot govern every entry, including normalization.
In the angular criteria, r is a theta-plane radius. For the small-o criterion,
the source cutoff is exactly ‖actualPairZero i‖≤1/r with real representatives;
their norm is the critical-line ordinate. Do not replace that cutoff by 1/√r.

PART V — THE RR SCALAR-PRODUCT FORMULATION
Derived mathematical reformulation; no native GP identification is claimed.
For each actual upper-half-plane zero occurrence j put δ_j=Re(ρ_j)−1/2 and
γ_j=Im(ρ_j)>0. Keep multiplicity either as repeated occurrences (m_j=1) or
as positive weights m_j; do not use both conventions simultaneously.
Assume the source mass summability Σ_j m_j/γ_j²<∞. On the disjoint union of
the positive t-axes define dν(j,t)=m_j t exp(−γ_j t)dt, A(j,t)=exp(iδ_j t), B=1.
Both A and B lie in L²(ν), since |A|=|B|=1 and ν(total)=Σ_j m_j/γ_j².
For each j, direct integration gives
    ∫₀∞ t e^(−γ_j t)(1−cos(δ_j t)) dt
    = 1/γ_j² − (γ_j²−δ_j²)/(γ_j²+δ_j²)²
    = δ_j²(3γ_j²+δ_j²)/(γ_j²(γ_j²+δ_j²)²).
Tonelli applied to the nonnegative integrand and |e^(iδt)−1|²=2(1−cos δt) gives
    C = (1/2) ||A−B||²_{L²(ν)}.
    C=0 ⇔ A=B ν-almost everywhere ⇔ every δ_j=0.
For δ_j≠0 the continuous nonnegative integrand is positive on a nonempty
interval, so its integral is strictly positive. This proves the last implication.
Using the actual-zero coverage in E23/E24, this
gives the same RH criterion as actual RR defect zero, not a new independent test.
Also C≤0 ⇔ C=0 because C≥0. Project candidate N03 asks precisely for C≤0.
To identify this L² space with the historical GP scalar product still requires
an explicit isometry / equality of the actual objects and normalization.
Equality at one anchor does not establish equality of all moments or a germ.
This formulation still uses actual zero data; it does not supply a zero-free
algorithm for computing C or proving its vanishing.
