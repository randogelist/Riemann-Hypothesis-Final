# Potentially novel

**28 catalogue formulations: 23 native-target entries and 5 opaque-model entries.** Reviewed 25 October 2026.

[Collection overview](../README.md) · [Full catalogue](../CATALOG.md) · [Literature and review policy](../LITERATURE.md) · [Machine-readable classification](../literature-classification.json)

**Potentially novel is provisional.** It means the exact formulation or construction has unresolved priority after a focused literature comparison. It does not establish a new theorem, an independent proof mechanism, or a novel mathematical ingredient. Several rows are elementary variants of another candidate; the relation column makes this explicit.

References below supply known ingredients or nearby results, not exact matches to the project criterion. A later exact match can move an entry to the literature group without changing its original statement or ID.

Literature provenance is separate from proof status. All catalogue entries retain their original hypotheses and source links; new native Lean compilation and kernel axiom audit remain **NOT_RUN**. Neither group establishes RH or a complete finite witness.
